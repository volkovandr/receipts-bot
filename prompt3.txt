You are extracting structured data from German retail receipts. Follow this process exactly.

=== STEP 0: ANALYZE RECEIPT STRUCTURE ===

Before processing any items:
1. Identify the price column (right-aligned numerical values)
2. Check for VAT indicators that appear AFTER prices:
   - Common indicators: 1, 2, A, B, a, b, *, †, ‡
   - Pattern: [PRICE][SPACE][VAT_INDICATOR]
   - Examples: "4.99 A", "12.50 1", "3.00 b", "7.95 *"
   - These indicators are NOT part of the price value
3. Detect quantity line pattern (scan entire receipt first):
   - Look for lines with quantity indicators (x, X, Stk, kg, St., etc.)
   - Determine if quantities appear ABOVE or BELOW item names
   - This pattern is CONSISTENT throughout the receipt
   - Lock this pattern for ALL subsequent processing

=== STEP 1: EXTRACT CORE INFORMATION ===

Extract merchant details and transaction data:
- Merchant: name, full address, city, country
- Transaction: date, time, totals (net/VAT/brutto)
- Payment: method, last 4 digits if card
- Identify all right-aligned prices (excluding VAT indicators)

=== STEP 2: IDENTIFY ITEMS ===

For each right-aligned price found:
1. The line with the price is the primary item line
2. Strip any VAT indicator from the price (e.g., "4.99 A" → 4.99)
3. Check for multi-line items:
   - Lines WITHOUT right-aligned prices may be continuations
   - Merge these with the item above
4. Apply the locked quantity pattern from Step 0:
   - If pattern is "below": check line below for quantity
   - If pattern is "above": check line above for quantity
   - NEVER switch patterns mid-receipt

=== STEP 3: PROCESS SPECIAL CASES ===

1. DEPOSITS (PFAND):
   - Always separate items with their own price
   - Common: "EINWEG PFAND", "MEHRWEG PFAND", "PFAND"
   - Include full amount (e.g., 0.25, 1.50)

2. DISCOUNTS:
   - Inline (no price): attach to item above
   - Separate (with price): keep as negative-priced item

3. QUANTITY LINES:
   - Format: "2 x 1.99 €" or "2 Stk" or "0.500 kg x 5.98 €/kg"
   - Calculate: quantity × unit_price = total_price
   - Verify math matches (tolerance ±0.02€)

=== STEP 4: VALIDATE AND CLEAN ===

1. Math verification:
   - Items with quantity > 1: verify quantity × unit_price = total_price
   - Sum of all items should ≈ transaction total (±0.50€)
2. Remove non-items:
   - Subtotals, tax breakdowns, payment confirmations
   - Card transaction details, receipt numbers
3. Format rules:
   - Quantity = 1: show ONLY total_price (no quantity/unit_price fields)
   - Quantity > 1: include quantity, unit_price, and total_price
   - Keep names in German, do not try to translate anything.

=== COMMON PATTERNS TO RECOGNIZE ===

Price with VAT indicator:
  MILCH           2.49 A    → price: 2.49, ignore 'A'
  BROT            1.99 1    → price: 1.99, ignore '1'

Quantity ABOVE item (if this is the detected pattern):
  2 Stk
  WASSER          3.98      → quantity: 2, unit_price: 1.99

Quantity BELOW item (if this is the detected pattern):
  WASSER          3.98
  2 x 1.99 €               → quantity: 2, unit_price: 1.99

Multi-line items:
  BIO VOLLMILCH   2.49 A    → name: "BIO VOLLMILCH 3.5% FETT"
  3.5% FETT                    (merge lines without prices)
